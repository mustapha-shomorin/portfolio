Featured project

NorthStar Retail: End-to-End Retail Data Platform

An end-to-end retail data engineering platform that brings together ingestion, storage, transformation, orchestration, data quality, monitoring and business intelligence to turn operational records into trusted commercial and operational insights.

Watch the 4-minute demo

Sources (historical batch data + synthetic order/customer events) → Ingestion (Python, Redpanda) → Bronze → Silver → Gold (DuckDB + dbt) → Serving (Streamlit executive dashboard), with Airflow orchestration and Prometheus/Grafana monitoring across the whole flow.

The problem

Retail teams struggle with fragmented operational data, limited visibility and untrusted reporting. The goal was a reliable data foundation that turns raw transactional records into insights for commercial and operational decisions.

Architecture

Bronze preserves ingested data, Silver standardises and validates it, and Gold provides business-ready fact tables, dimensions and analytical marts. Data is ingested in batch from a historical retail dataset, while synthetic order and customer activity events demonstrate the streaming pipeline.

Tech stack

Python Redpanda DuckDB dbt Airflow Streamlit Prometheus Grafana Docker Object storage

Reliability & data quality

Airflow orchestrates ingestion, transformation, validation and final analytical build, and stops downstream processing when required upstream tasks fail. Includes retry behaviour, failure notifications, replay protection and automated quality checks for critical fields, uniqueness, referential integrity and business rules.

Monitoring

A Grafana system-health dashboard (Prometheus metrics) tracks latest pipeline status, failures, records processed, data freshness and the health of the metrics service itself.

Executive dashboard

A Streamlit dashboard exposes the Gold layer through five sections: revenue performance, geographic revenue comparison, product category performance, customer value and operations.

What I demonstrate

End-to-end data engineering workflow, reproducible processing, dimensional modelling, automated quality checks, orchestration and system monitoring.
